Four tools. One practical journey.

You won't study Power Platform as four disconnected products. You'll understand how each piece fits into a simple business solution.

Power Apps

Start building canvas apps, work with forms and galleries, connect data, and learn the basics of Power Fx.

Power Automate

Create flows, notifications and approvals — then connect automation back to your apps and business data.

Power BI

Work with Excel, SharePoint, folders and other beginner-friendly data sources to clean, model and visualize data.

Copilot Studio

Create your first AI agent, add knowledge, design conversations and understand how agents can help users.

What happens over 6 weeks?

The scope is deliberately beginner-first. We build confidence before complexity. Advanced Power Platform comes later.

WEEK 01

Power Apps Foundations

BUILD
  • Power Platform overview
  • Canvas Apps & Power Apps Studio
  • Screens, controls, forms & galleries
  • Navigation and basic Power Fx
WEEK 02

Power Apps → Automation

CONNECT
  • Excel & SharePoint data
  • Create, edit, search & filter records
  • Variables and basic formulas
  • Connect the app to Power Automate
WEEK 03

Power Automate

AUTOMATE
  • Cloud flows, triggers & actions
  • Conditions & dynamic content
  • Emails, notifications & approvals
  • App → Flow business process
WEEK 04

Power Automate → BI

INSIGHT
  • Automated processes around reporting
  • Working with Power BI-connected processes
  • Basic monitoring and flow history
  • Understand the full business workflow
WEEK 05

Power BI

ANALYZE
  • Excel, SharePoint, folders & data sources
  • Power Query basics
  • Data cleaning and simple modeling
  • Charts, KPIs, filters & slicers
WEEK 06

Copilot Studio

ADD AI
  • What is an AI agent?
  • Create and configure an agent
  • Knowledge, topics & generative answers
  • Connect the agent to simple business actions
